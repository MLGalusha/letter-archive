# About This Project

## What Is Letter Archive?

A platform for preserving and exploring historical letter collections. Transforms physical letters into a digital archive where visitors can discover stories and connections.

## The Vision: A Wise Guide

The ultimate goal is an AI experience that feels like talking to **someone who knows all the stories**.

"What letters do you have from the Civil War?"
"Tell me about someone who experienced loss."
"What was happening in 1942?"

Not database queries—thoughtful answers from a guide who has read every letter.

## Who It's For

**The Collector:** Has letters from many eras, wants them seen and explored, not hidden in boxes.

**The Visitors:** History enthusiasts, researchers, genealogists, anyone who loves personal correspondence.

## Core Goals

1. **Preservation** — Digitize, transcribe (AI + human verified), catalogue
2. **Explorability** — Search, filter, see originals, follow stories across letters
3. **Story Connections** — Track relationships, events, themes across collections
4. **Accessibility** — Transcriptions make handwriting readable, AI guide lowers barriers

## What Success Looks Like

A permanent, beautiful home that makes exploration compelling. Enough engagement to potentially attract funding for continued archiving.

## Technical Implications

| Goal | Need |
|------|------|
| See originals | High-quality image storage/viewing |
| Search many dimensions | Rich metadata extraction |
| AI guide | Vector embeddings, semantic search |
| Story connections | Relationship tracking |
| Transcription | AI + human verification workflow |

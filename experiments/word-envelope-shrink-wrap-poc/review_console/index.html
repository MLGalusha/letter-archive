First-use guide

You are taking the agent’s seat

Software proposes the next word region. Your job is to make that viewport contain one complete word, select its exact ink, fit the envelope, and only then erase it.

  1. 1
    Orient yourself.

    Confirm the page, line, word, model tier, and revision at the top. The left rail always names the current and next step.

  2. 2
    Judge the proposal.

    The rectangle is only a starting viewport. Decide whether it contains one complete word, a clipped word, several words, partial letters, or touching ink.

  3. 3
    Select approximate, then refine.

    Compare the original, clean ink, and strong ink in identical coordinates. Drag around the word for the first selection, then click individual pieces to add or remove them until exactly one word remains.

  4. 4
    Fit, inspect, erase.

    Run the deterministic envelope, inspect its safety metrics, then approve it. The claimed word disappears from the remaining-page view before software loads the next proposal.
